## 2. Business context

- **Problem — the spreadsheet fails:** desk booking runs through a shared Google Sheet that breaks down weekly. `[CTX · Current state]` "people book the same desk twice, forget to release a booking when plans change, and there's no visibility into which floor is actually full"
- **Problem — the spreadsheet is bypassed:** `[W1 · Discussion]` "people have also started just claiming a desk in person and ignoring the sheet entirely"
- **Why now:** the new hybrid-work policy needs self-service availability. `[CTX · Why now]` "assumes desk availability is self-service"
- **Constraint on the solution:** a light PoC, because internal IT has no capacity. `[HO · Sales handoff notes (verbatim from the account exec)]` "IT is thin — no time for a real dev project, which is why they're open to a lightweight PoC"
- **Definition of done:** `[CTX · What "done" looks like for this engagement]` "a working PoC that lets an employee book a desk by date and floor, see remaining capacity, and cancel a booking"
- **Success signal:** the sponsor can demo it to leadership, with the agreed scope only. `[CTX · What "done" looks like for this engagement]` "good enough for Priya to demo to her leadership as proof the idea works"; `[W2 · Decisions]` "Priya will demo the PoC to her leadership using this exact scope"
- **Delivery milestone:** `[BR · Next milestone]` "present results: the workflow, the backlog and a PoC demo" on 28 October 2026.
