Workshop 4 — Booking note

Date: 2026-10-24 Attendees: Priya Nandakumar (sponsor), Dana Whitfield, Sam

Priya: "People keep asking if they can leave a short note with a booking — like 'need a second monitor' or 'visiting from another office'." Dana: "Fine, as long as it is optional and short. No names of other people in it." Sam: "Keep it to 50 characters and keep it in the browser like everything else."

Agreed: an optional note per booking, maximum 50 characters. The note is shown on the booking confirmation and in the desk dialog. It is deleted together with the name after the booking date.
