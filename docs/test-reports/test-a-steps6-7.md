## 2. Findings

### B-4 — US-005 built without seeing its tests fail

In the batch run, the code of US-005 (storage `book()` and the booking form) was written before its unit and E2E tests, so those tests were never seen red. From US-016 on, every story was tests first and failed for the right reason before the code; US-015 is a verification story with no new code (tests green on the first run). Recorded in `build-status.md` B-4. Impact: low — the US-005 behaviour is also covered by later stories' tests (US-001, US-006, US-015), but the "tests first" rule of the `build` skill was broken once.

### K-14 — a gate the BA waives must be recorded in `approvals` automatically (kit)

**What happened.** The BA said "сделай все стори и закомить их" — build every remaining story and commit. That waived the H6 decision point (BA accepts each story before the next) for US-005…US-013. The `build` skill had no rule for this: the waiver was recorded only as a note in `build-status.md` ("BA check pending"), and the `approvals` entry was added a day later, when the BA asked for it.

**Why it matters.** `approvals` is the audit trail of BA decisions (`ba-rules` §4, §5). A gate that is skipped without a trace looks like a gate Claude skipped on its own.

**Proposal for the kit.** In `ba-rules` §4 (and `build` §Rules): when the BA waives a decision point in the conversation ("build everything", "don't stop", "commit each"), the step skill appends at once `{step, status: gate-waived, by: <people.ba>, date, note: "<gate> waived — <BA's words>; <scope>"}` to `approvals`, marks the affected artifacts "built without BA check — pending", and asks for the deferred check when the run ends.
