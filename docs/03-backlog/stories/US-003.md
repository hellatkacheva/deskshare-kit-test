## Story
As an employee, I want to choose a date and a floor and see how many desks are booked and left, so that I know where I can sit before I book.

## Context and business rules
1. Two floors only: Floor 1 and Floor 2. — REQ-003 `[W2 · Booking a desk]` "Two floors only for the PoC"
2. Capacity: Floor 1 = 30 desks, Floor 2 = 25 desks, the same on every day. — REQ-004, REQ-005 "Floor 1 (capacity 30)", "Floor 2 (capacity 25)"; ASM-002 (ASSUMPTION — not validated)
3. Bookable dates: today and up to 6 days ahead; weekends and past dates cannot be chosen. — ASM-010 (ASSUMPTION — not validated)
4. For the chosen date the screen shows, per floor, the number of booked desks and of remaining desks (remaining = capacity − booked). — REQ-009, REQ-010
5. Counts only — no names on this view. — NFR-002, NFR-003
6. Counts are computed from the bookings in this browser. — DEC-014, ASM-006
7. On opening the app the selected date is today; on Saturday or Sunday the next working day. — REQ-039, DEC-022
8. "Today" is the date in the office time zone (UTC+5 for the demo), from the device clock. — NFR-009, NFR-010, DEC-016, ASM-017 (ASSUMPTION — not validated)
9. While the app is open, counts, "today" and the default date are re-evaluated on every user action and at least once a minute. — REQ-042, DEC-028

## UI notes
Date picker, floor selector (Floor 1 / Floor 2), a counts line per floor. The wording of the counts line is a UI choice; the numbers are the rule.
