## Story
As an employee, I want to see by when I must check in, so that I keep my desk.

## Context and business rules
1. Every booking gets a check-in deadline: 10:00 office time on the booking day. — ASM-008 (ASSUMPTION — not validated), DEC-008 `[W3 · No-show / ghost bookings (new ask from Priya)]` "a booking auto-releases if not confirmed by a cutoff time"
2. For a same-day booking made after 09:30, the deadline is the earlier of (booking time + 30 min) and 23:59 of the booking day. — ASM-008, REQ-041, DEC-024
3. From 23:59 office time a booking for today is refused with "Booking for today is closed — please pick another date"; other dates are not limited. — REQ-041, DEC-024
4. All times are office time (UTC+5 for the demo, from the device clock) and are shown with the label, e.g. "10:00 office time (UTC+5)". Times are shown in two places: the booking confirmation and the check-in field (US-008). — NFR-009, NFR-010, DEC-016, ASM-017 (ASSUMPTION — not validated)
5. The confirmation (US-005) also shows the deadline. — REQ-036, DEC-019
