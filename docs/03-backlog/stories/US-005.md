## Story
As an employee, I want to book a free desk for a date by typing my name, so that I have a desk without waiting for anyone's approval.

## Context and business rules
1. A booking = date + desk (which fixes the floor) + name typed as free text; no account, no login. — REQ-006 `[W2 · Out of scope, explicitly confirmed]` "name is typed in at booking time"; REQ-022
2. The booking is confirmed at once: no approval step and no pending state. — REQ-007 "no approval step"; NFR-006; ASM-016 (ASSUMPTION — not validated: "immediately" = no pending or approval state)
3. Only a free desk can be booked; a desk already booked for that date is refused. — REQ-023
4. **State effect:** after booking, the desk is taken for that date; the floor's booked count goes up by 1 and remaining goes down by 1. — REQ-008, REQ-023
5. One booking per name per day; a second booking under the same name for the same date is refused with the message "This name already has a booking for that day." — ASM-011 (ASSUMPTION — not validated). Names are compared case-insensitively, ignoring leading, trailing and repeated spaces. — REQ-035, DEC-018
6. The date must be inside the booking window (US-003 rule 3). — ASM-010 (ASSUMPTION — not validated)
7. The booking gets a check-in deadline: 10:00 on the booking day; for a same-day booking made after 09:30, the earlier of (booking time + 30 min) and 23:59 of the booking day. — ASM-008 (ASSUMPTION — not validated), REQ-041, DEC-024
8. From 23:59 office time a booking for today is refused with "Booking for today is closed — please pick another date"; other dates are not limited. — REQ-041, DEC-024
9. All times are office time (UTC+5 for the demo) and shown with the zone label, e.g. "10:00 office time (UTC+5)". — NFR-009, DEC-016, ASM-017 (ASSUMPTION — not validated)
10. The name is stored with the booking only for the booking itself. — NFR-004 `[W2 · Privacy]` "Booking records tied to a name for the purpose of the booking itself is fine"
11. Name rules: a name of spaces only counts as empty and is refused; at most 50 characters; shown as plain text, never as markup. — REQ-035, DEC-018, ASM-018 (ASSUMPTION — not validated)
12. The confirmation shows the date, the desk number and the check-in deadline, plus the hint "Note your desk number — you need it to check in or cancel". There is no search of bookings by name. — REQ-036, DEC-019
