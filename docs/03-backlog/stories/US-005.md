## Story
As an employee, I want to book a free desk for a date by typing my name, so that I have a desk without waiting for anyone's approval.

## Context and business rules
1. A booking = date + desk (which fixes the floor) + name typed as free text; no account, no login. — REQ-006 `[W2 · Out of scope, explicitly confirmed]` "name is typed in at booking time"; REQ-022
2. The booking is confirmed at once: no approval step and no pending state. — REQ-007 "no approval step"; NFR-006; ASM-016 (ASSUMPTION — not validated: "immediately" = no pending or approval state)
3. Only a free desk can be booked; a desk already booked for that date is refused. — REQ-023
4. **State effect:** after booking, the desk is taken for that date; the floor's booked count goes up by 1 and remaining goes down by 1. — REQ-008, REQ-023
5. One booking per name per day; a second booking under the same name for the same date is refused with "This name already has a booking for that day." Names are compared case-insensitively, ignoring leading, trailing and repeated spaces. — ASM-011 (ASSUMPTION — not validated), REQ-035, DEC-018
6. The date must be inside the booking window (US-003 rule 3); a booking for a date outside it is refused at save, also when the screen was opened earlier. — ASM-010 (ASSUMPTION — not validated)
7. At the moment of saving, the app re-reads the bookings in this browser and re-checks rules 3, 5, 6 and the full floor (US-006) — another tab may have changed them. — REQ-023, REQ-011 (finding #11)
8. The name is stored with the booking only for the booking itself. — NFR-004 `[W2 · Privacy]` "Booking records tied to a name for the purpose of the booking itself is fine"
9. The confirmation shows the date and the desk number, plus the hint "Note your desk number — you need it to check in or cancel"; it does **not** show the name. The check-in deadline is added by US-017. There is no search of bookings by name. — REQ-036, DEC-019
10. Name validation: US-016. Check-in deadline and closing of same-day booking: US-017.
