## Story
As an employee, I want desks of no-show bookings to become free automatically, so that I am not turned away from a desk that would stay empty.

## Context and business rules
1. A booking not checked in by its deadline is released automatically. — REQ-017 `[W3 · No-show / ghost bookings (new ask from Priya)]` "a booking auto-releases if not confirmed by a cutoff time"
2. Deadline as in US-017 (10:00, or for same-day bookings after 09:30 the earlier of booking time + 30 min and 23:59). The booking is released **at** the deadline (check-in only strictly before it). — ASM-008 (ASSUMPTION — not validated), REQ-041, DEC-024, REQ-037, DEC-020
3. There is no server or background job (DEC-014): release is evaluated whenever bookings are read — on every user action and at least once a minute while the app is open. — DEC-014, REQ-042, DEC-028
4. **State effect:** the released booking is removed; the desk is free at once; booked goes down by 1 and remaining goes up by 1. — REQ-018
5. A released desk can be booked again the same day, also under the same name. — ASM-009, ASM-011 (ASSUMPTION — not validated)
6. A checked-in booking is never released. — REQ-016
7. Times are office time (UTC+5 for the demo), from the device clock; tests set "now" with the test-only clock override. — NFR-009, NFR-010, DEC-016, ASM-017 (ASSUMPTION — not validated)

## Acceptance criteria
```gherkin
Scenario: Booking released after the deadline (state effect)
  Given "Jamie Lee" booked F2-03 for Thu 2026-10-22, not checked in, deadline 10:00
  And on 2026-10-22 Floor 2 has 25 booked and 0 left
  When the app shows Floor 2 for 2026-10-22 at 10:01
  Then F2-03 is free
  And Floor 2 shows 24 booked and 1 left

Scenario: Released exactly at the deadline (boundary)
  Given "Jamie Lee"'s booking of F2-03 on 2026-10-22 is not checked in, deadline 10:00
  When the app shows Floor 2 at 10:00
  Then F2-03 is free

Scenario: Not released before the deadline
  Given "Jamie Lee"'s booking of F2-03 on 2026-10-22 is not checked in
  When the app shows Floor 2 at 09:59
  Then F2-03 is still taken

Scenario: Released without anyone using the app at 10:00
  Given the app was closed from 09:00 to 11:00 on 2026-10-22
  And "Jamie Lee"'s booking of F2-03 was not checked in
  When the app is opened at 11:00
  Then F2-03 is shown as free for 2026-10-22

Scenario: Page left open with no interaction (DEC-028)
  Given the app shows Floor 2 for 2026-10-22 at 09:59 with F2-03 taken and not checked in
  When nobody touches the page until 10:01
  Then at 10:01 F2-03 is shown as free and Floor 2 shows 1 more desk left

Scenario: Checked-in booking is not released
  Given "Alex Morgan" checked in to F1-07 on 2026-10-22 at 09:10
  When the app shows Floor 1 for 2026-10-22 at 10:01
  Then F1-07 is still taken

Scenario: Released desk can be booked again
  Given F2-03 was released at 10:00 on 2026-10-22
  When "Sam Rivera" books F2-03 for 2026-10-22 at 10:05
  Then the booking is confirmed with deadline 10:35

Scenario: Same name can book again after release
  Given "Jamie Lee"'s booking of F2-03 on 2026-10-22 was released at 10:00
  When "Jamie Lee" books F2-05 for 2026-10-22 at 10:10
  Then the booking is confirmed

Scenario: Late same-day booking released at 23:59
  Given "Jamie Lee" booked F2-03 for Wed 2026-10-21 at 23:45 (deadline 23:59) and did not check in
  When the app shows Floor 2 for 2026-10-21 at 23:59
  Then F2-03 is free
```
