## Story
As an employee, I want to cancel my own booking myself, so that the desk is free for someone else when my plans change.

## Context and business rules
1. The employee cancels without the Office Manager or IT. — REQ-013 `[W2 · Cancelling]` "cancel their own booking without going through Priya or IT"
2. "Own" booking: the employee selects the date and the taken desk and re-types the name; the name must match the booking case-insensitively, ignoring leading, trailing and repeated spaces. — ASM-012 (ASSUMPTION — not validated)
3. Cancel is allowed up to the end of the booking day, also after check-in. — ASM-012 (ASSUMPTION — not validated)
4. **State effect:** the booking is removed; the desk is free at once; the floor's booked count goes down by 1 and remaining goes up by 1. — REQ-014, ASM-012
5. Known limitation: anyone who knows the name can cancel the booking. — DEC-012, RSK-003
6. The self-reported notice is shown on the cancel name field (US-012). — REQ-040, DEC-023
