## Story
As an employee, I want to confirm (check in) my booking on the day, so that my desk is not released as a no-show.

## Context and business rules
1. Check-in confirms a booking. — REQ-015 `[W3 · No-show / ghost bookings (new ask from Priya)]` "add a check-in/grace-period mechanic"
2. Check-in is possible only on the booking day, without any presence check. — ASM-009 (ASSUMPTION — not validated)
3. Identification as for cancel: date + taken desk + re-typed name, case-insensitive, ignoring leading, trailing and repeated spaces. — ASM-012, ASM-009 (ASSUMPTION — not validated)
4. Check-in is accepted only **strictly before** the booking's deadline (deadline: US-017). At the deadline it is too late. — ASM-008 (ASSUMPTION — not validated), REQ-037, DEC-020
5. **State effect:** the booking is marked checked in and is never auto-released. — REQ-016
6. A booking that is already checked in cannot be checked in again; it stays checked in.
7. Times are office time (UTC+5 for the demo), from the device clock. — NFR-009, NFR-010, DEC-016, ASM-017 (ASSUMPTION — not validated)
8. The self-reported notice is shown next to the check-in name field. — REQ-040, DEC-023

## Acceptance criteria

Scenario: Check in before the deadline (happy path)
  Given "Alex Morgan" booked F1-07 for Thu 2026-10-22 with deadline 10:00
  And now is 2026-10-22 09:10
  When the employee selects F1-07, types "Alex Morgan" and checks in
  Then the booking is checked in

Scenario: State effect of check-in
  Given "Alex Morgan" checked in to F1-07 on 2026-10-22 at 09:10
  When the page is reloaded
  Then the booking of F1-07 is still checked in and F1-07 is taken

Scenario: Check-in before the booking day is refused
  Given "Alex Morgan" booked F1-07 for Thu 2026-10-22
  And now is Wed 2026-10-21 16:00
  When the employee tries to check in F1-07 for 2026-10-22
  Then the check-in is refused with a message that check-in is possible only on the booking day

Scenario: Wrong name
  Given "Alex Morgan" booked F1-07 for 2026-10-22 and now is 2026-10-22 09:10
  When the employee types "Jamie Lee" and checks in F1-07
  Then the check-in is refused and the booking stays not checked in

Scenario: Name match ignores case and extra spaces
  Given "Alex Morgan" booked F1-07 for 2026-10-22 and now is 2026-10-22 09:10
  When the employee types "  ALEX   morgan" and checks in F1-07
  Then the booking is checked in

Scenario: One minute before the deadline (boundary)
  Given the deadline of F1-07 on 2026-10-22 is 10:00 and now is 09:59
  When "Alex Morgan" checks in
  Then the booking is checked in

Scenario: Exactly at the deadline is too late (boundary)
  Given the deadline of F1-07 on 2026-10-22 is 10:00 and now is 10:00
  When "Alex Morgan" tries to check in
  Then the check-in is refused

Scenario: Second check-in
  Given "Alex Morgan" checked in to F1-07 on 2026-10-22 at 09:10
  When "Alex Morgan" checks in F1-07 again at 09:20

  And the booking stays checked in
