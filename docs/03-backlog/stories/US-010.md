## Story
As the sponsor at the demo, I want one switch that turns colleagues' names on and off, so that I can show both variants to leadership and make the scope call.

## Context and business rules
1. Switch label: **"Show colleagues' names: Off / On"**. — REQ-025, DEC-011
2. Default position: **Off** (Variant A, US-004). — REQ-025, DEC-011
3. Off → Variant A: taken desks show "taken", no names, no consent checkbox. On → Variant B (US-011). — DEC-011
4. Switching does not change any booking or consent stored with a booking. — DEC-011
5. After every page reload the switch is Off again. — REQ-038, DEC-021

## Acceptance criteria
```gherkin
Scenario: Default is Off
  Given the app is opened for the first time in this browser
  Then the switch "Show colleagues' names" is Off
  And taken desks show "taken" without names

Scenario: Turn On and Off
  Given the switch is Off
  When the presenter turns it On
  Then the grid renders Variant B (US-011)
  When the presenter turns it Off
  Then the grid renders Variant A (US-004)

Scenario: Switching keeps bookings (state effect)
  Given Floor 1 shows 13 booked and 17 left for 2026-10-22
  When the presenter turns the switch On and then Off
  Then Floor 1 still shows 13 booked and 17 left
  And every booking keeps its consent value

Scenario: Off again after a reload
  Given the presenter turned the switch On
  When the page is reloaded
  Then the switch is Off
  And taken desks show "taken" without names
```
