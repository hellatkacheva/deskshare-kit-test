## Story
As the HR Business Partner, I want names on bookings to be removed once the booking day is over, so that the tool does not build up an attendance history.

## Context and business rules
1. Names are deleted after the booking date; only per-floor counts per date are kept (booked bookings that were not cancelled or released). — ASM-014 (ASSUMPTION — not validated), DEC-029
2. Purpose: no individual attendance history that could be pulled into a report. — NFR-002 `[W2 · Privacy]` "should not surface anyone's individual attendance history in a way that could be pulled into a report"
3. With no server (DEC-014), the deletion runs when the app is opened or bookings are read on a later date; "after the booking date" is judged in the office time zone (UTC+5 for the demo). — DEC-014, DEC-016, NFR-009, RSK-008
4. Kept counts are not shown in any view (past dates cannot be selected — ASM-010).
5. Must story: it is not dropped without a recorded BA decision (review finding #3).
