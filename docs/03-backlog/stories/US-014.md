# US-014 — Names removed after the booking date

## Story
As the HR Business Partner, I want names on bookings to be removed once the booking day is over, so that the tool does not build up an attendance history.

## Context and business rules
1. Names are deleted after the booking date; only per-floor counts per date are kept. — ASM-014 (ASSUMPTION — not validated)
2. Purpose: no individual attendance history that could be pulled into a report. — NFR-002 `[W2 · Privacy]` "should not surface anyone's individual attendance history in a way that could be pulled into a report"
3. With no server (DEC-014), the deletion runs when the app is opened or bookings are read on a later date; "after the booking date" is judged in the office time zone (UTC+5 for the demo). — DEC-014, DEC-016, NFR-009, RSK-008
4. Kept counts are not shown in any view (past dates cannot be selected — ASM-010).

## Acceptance criteria
```gherkin
Scenario: Names of past days are removed (state effect)
  Given on Wed 2026-10-21 bookings exist for 2026-10-21 under "Alex Morgan" (F1-07) and "Jamie Lee" (F2-03)
  When the app is opened on Thu 2026-10-22
  Then the browser storage holds no name for any booking dated 2026-10-21
  And it holds for 2026-10-21 only the count of booked desks per floor: Floor 1 = 1, Floor 2 = 1

Scenario: Today's and future names are kept
  Given "Alex Morgan" booked F1-07 for 2026-10-22
  When the app is opened on 2026-10-22
  Then the booking of F1-07 for 2026-10-22 still has the name "Alex Morgan"

Scenario: Removal also after days without use
  Given bookings with names exist for 2026-10-21 and 2026-10-22
  And the app is not opened until Mon 2026-10-26
  When the app is opened on 2026-10-26
  Then no booking dated before 2026-10-26 holds a name
```
