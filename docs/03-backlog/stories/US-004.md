## Story
As an employee, I want to see the desks of the selected floor for the selected date as a grid of free and taken desks, so that I can pick a specific free desk.

## Context and business rules
1. Desks: Floor 1 = F1-01 … F1-30, Floor 2 = F2-01 … F2-25, shown as a simple grid; no floor plan. — ASM-007 (ASSUMPTION — not validated), DEC-015
2. A desk is **free** if it has no booking for the selected date, otherwise **taken**. — REQ-022, REQ-023
3. **Variant A (names switch Off — the default):** a taken desk shows "taken", without a name. — REQ-026, DEC-011
4. A floor is full for a date when all its desks are taken. — REQ-024
5. Taken desks stay selectable, because cancel and check-in start from the desk (US-007, US-008). — ASM-012

## Acceptance criteria
```gherkin
Scenario: Grid shows all desks of the floor
  Given the employee selected Floor 2 and Thu 2026-10-22
  Then the grid shows the 25 desks F2-01 … F2-25

Scenario: Taken desk without a name (Variant A)
  Given "Alex Morgan" booked F1-07 for 2026-10-22
  And the names switch is Off
  When the employee views Floor 1 for 2026-10-22
  Then F1-07 is shown as "taken"
  And the name "Alex Morgan" is not shown anywhere on the screen

Scenario: Grid follows the date
  Given F1-07 is booked for 2026-10-22 only
  When the employee selects 2026-10-23
  Then F1-07 is shown as free

Scenario: Full floor
  Given all 25 desks of Floor 2 are booked for 2026-10-22
  When the employee views Floor 2 for 2026-10-22
  Then no desk of Floor 2 is shown as free
```

## UI notes
Each tile shows the desk number and its state (free / taken). Names on tiles in Variant B: US-011.
