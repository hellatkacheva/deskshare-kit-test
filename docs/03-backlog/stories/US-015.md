## Story
As the sponsor, I want the demo flow checked end to end on a phone and a desktop browser, so that I can show it to leadership on 28 October 2026 without surprises.

## Context and business rules
1. Runs in a web browser, layout works on mobile; no native app. — NFR-001 `[W2 · Out of scope, explicitly confirmed]` "responsive browser use is enough for the demo"
2. Phone check: 375 × 667 viewport, Chrome Android and Safari iOS; desktop: Chrome and Edge — each in its latest stable version on the test date. — ASM-016 (ASSUMPTION — not validated)
3. The demo flow is the definition of done (book a desk by date and floor, see remaining capacity, cancel) plus the Core scope: hard stop, check-in, names switch. — NFR-007 `[CTX · What "done" looks like for this engagement]`, DEC-025, DEC-026
4. Demo date: 28 October 2026 (`project.deadline`). — CST-007, DEC-027
5. **Demo-day reset:** on the demo device, on the demo day, between 08:00 and 09:30 office time, clear the site data of the app in the browser and open the app — then D1 has the full Floor 2 and today's unconfirmed bookings have the deadline 10:00. Steps per browser: `README.md` and the demo-day checklist (`docs/04-spec/demo-data.md` §9). — REQ-034, REQ-044, DEC-017, DEC-031, ASM-008
6. The test-only clock override and `?reset-demo` have no effect in the demo build. — NFR-010, REQ-044, DEC-031
7. Tap targets are at least 44 × 44 CSS px on the phone viewport (WCAG 2.1 SC 2.5.5).

## Acceptance criteria
```gherkin
Scenario Outline: Core demo flow
  Given the site data of the app was cleared and the app opened at 08:30 office time on the test day, in <browser>
  When the presenter selects a date and a floor, picks a free desk on the grid, books it as "Alex Morgan" and then cancels it
  Then the remaining count goes down by 1 after booking and back up by 1 after cancel
  And the next working day shows Floor 2 full and a new booking there is refused with the "pick another day or floor" message
  And a seeded unconfirmed booking for today can be checked in before 10:00
  And turning "Show colleagues' names" On shows the names of consenting seed bookings, Off hides them
  And the banner "Demo — bookings are stored in this browser only." and the self-reported notice on the booking form are visible
  Examples:
    | browser                    |
    | Chrome Android, 375 × 667  |
    | Safari iOS, 375 × 667      |
    | Chrome desktop             |
    | Edge desktop               |

Scenario: No horizontal scrolling on the phone
  Given the 375 × 667 viewport
  When each screen of the app is shown (counts and grid, booking form, cancel, check-in, "Tomorrow")
  Then the page does not scroll horizontally
  And every button, tile and switch is at least 44 × 44 CSS px

  Given the demo build is opened with the test-only clock override set to 2026-10-22 09:00
  Then "today" is the real date of the device clock in the office time zone

  Given the demo build holds a booking made by the presenter in this browser
  When the demo build is opened with "?reset-demo"
  Then the booking is still there
```
