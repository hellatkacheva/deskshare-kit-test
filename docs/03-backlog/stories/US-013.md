## Story
As the Office Manager, I want one screen that shows whether tomorrow is a problem, so that I see a full or nearly full floor before the day starts.

## Context and business rules
1. Source: `[BR · Stakeholders (as quoted on the slides)]` "I want one screen that tells me if tomorrow's a problem."
2. The screen shows the **next working day**: Mon–Thu → the next day; Friday, Saturday and Sunday → Monday. — REQ-030, DEC-013
3. Per floor: booked / capacity (Floor 1: 30, Floor 2: 25). — REQ-030, REQ-004, REQ-005
4. Status per floor — occupancy = booked ÷ capacity: **green** below 80 %; **amber** from 80 % while desks are left; **red** when the floor is full. — REQ-030, DEC-013
   - Floor 1: green 0–23, amber 24–29, red 30.
   - Floor 2: green 0–19, amber 20–24, red 25.
5. Counts only, no names — also with the names switch On. — REQ-030, NFR-002
6. "Today" is the date in the office time zone (UTC+5 for the demo), from the device clock. — NFR-009, NFR-010, DEC-016, ASM-017 (ASSUMPTION — not validated)

## Acceptance criteria
```gherkin
Scenario: Tomorrow on a weekday
  Given today is Wed 2026-10-21
  When the Office Manager opens "Tomorrow"
  Then the screen shows Thu 2026-10-22

Scenario: Tomorrow on Friday is Monday
  Given today is Fri 2026-10-23
  When the Office Manager opens "Tomorrow"
  Then the screen shows Mon 2026-10-26

Scenario: Tomorrow on Saturday is Monday
  Given today is Sat 2026-10-24
  Then "Tomorrow" shows Mon 2026-10-26

Scenario: Floor 1 just below 80 % (boundary)
  Given on 2026-10-22 Floor 1 has 23 of 30 booked
  Then Floor 1 shows 23 / 30 and status green

Scenario: Floor 1 at 80 % (boundary)
  Given on 2026-10-22 Floor 1 has 24 of 30 booked
  Then Floor 1 shows 24 / 30 and status amber

Scenario: Floor 2 one desk left
  Given on 2026-10-22 Floor 2 has 24 of 25 booked
  Then Floor 2 shows 24 / 25 and status amber

Scenario: Floor 2 full
  Given on 2026-10-22 Floor 2 has 25 of 25 booked
  Then Floor 2 shows 25 / 25 and status red

Scenario: Status follows a cancel (state effect)
  Given on 2026-10-22 Floor 2 has 25 of 25 booked (red)
  When one booking on Floor 2 for 2026-10-22 is cancelled
  Then "Tomorrow" shows Floor 2 24 / 25 and status amber

Scenario: No names
  Given the names switch is On and bookings with consent exist for 2026-10-22
  Then the "Tomorrow" screen shows no names
```

## UI notes
One screen, two floor tiles with booked / capacity and a coloured status; a text label next to the colour (green / amber / red) so the status does not depend on colour alone.

## Out of scope for this story
