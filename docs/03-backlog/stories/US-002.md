## Story
As the presenter of the demo, I want the app to start with fictional bookings of other employees, so that remaining capacity, a full floor and the other features are visible on one device.

## Context and business rules
1. The PoC does not import the Google Sheet; it starts with seed data. — ASM-004 (ASSUMPTION — not validated)
2. Seed bookings use fictional names only. — REQ-032
3. Seed bookings use desks F1-01 … F1-30 and F2-01 … F2-25. — ASM-007 (ASSUMPTION — not validated)
4. Seed bookings follow all booking rules: booking window, one booking per name per day, one booking per desk per date. — ASM-010, ASM-011, REQ-023
5. Seed dates are relative to today (office time zone, DEC-016): D0 = today, D1 and D2 = the next 2 working days; on Saturday or Sunday D0 = the next working day. — REQ-033, DEC-017
6. D0: some bookings checked in and some not checked in; the unconfirmed ones are created as same-day bookings at seed time, so their deadline follows US-017: seeded at or before 09:30 → 10:00; seeded after 09:30 → seed time + 30 min (at most 23:59). If seeded on a weekend, no D0 booking is checked in. — REQ-033, DEC-017, ASM-008 (ASSUMPTION — not validated)
7. D1: Floor 1 = 25 of 30 booked (amber on "Tomorrow"), Floor 2 = 25 of 25 booked (full, red). — REQ-033, DEC-017
8. Some seed bookings have name consent (for Variant B). — REQ-033, DEC-017
9. The seed is loaded on first open and by a team-only reset: opening the app with the URL parameter `?reset-demo` deletes all DeskShare data in this browser and loads the seed again. After the reset the parameter is removed from the address bar, so a later reload does not reset again. There is no visible reset button. — REQ-034, DEC-017 (finding #10)
    **`?reset-demo` works only in the local / test build** (`localhost`, `127.0.0.1`); the deployed build ignores it. In the deployed build the team resets by clearing the site data in the browser; the app then loads the seed as on first open. `README.md` and the demo-day checklist describe this. — REQ-044, DEC-031
10. The demo-day reset procedure is part of US-015.
11. Exact seed rows (86): `docs/04-spec/demo-data.md`. — DEC-032

## Acceptance criteria
```gherkin
Scenario: First open loads the seed
  Given this browser has no DeskShare data
  When the app is opened
  Then the seed bookings are loaded
  And every seed booking has a fictional name, a desk from F1-01…F1-30 or F2-01…F2-25 and a date inside the booking window

Scenario: Seed does not overwrite existing data
  Given this browser already has DeskShare data
  When the app is opened without "?reset-demo"
  Then the existing data is kept unchanged

Scenario: Seed content relative to today
  Given now is Wed 2026-10-21 08:00 office time
  When the seed is loaded
  Then on Thu 2026-10-22 Floor 1 has 25 of 30 booked and Floor 2 has 25 of 25 booked
  And on Wed 2026-10-21 some bookings are checked in and some are not
  And on 2026-10-21 or 2026-10-22 at least one booking has name consent

Scenario: Deadline of seeded unconfirmed bookings — seeded before 09:30
  Given now is Wed 2026-10-21 08:00 office time
  When the seed is loaded
  Then every unconfirmed booking for 2026-10-21 has the deadline 10:00

Scenario: Deadline of seeded unconfirmed bookings — seeded after 09:30
  Given now is Wed 2026-10-21 11:00 office time
  When the seed is loaded
  Then every unconfirmed booking for 2026-10-21 has the deadline 11:30

Scenario: Seed follows the booking rules
  When the seed is loaded
  Then no desk has two bookings on the same date
  And no name has two bookings on the same date

Scenario: Team-only reset in the local / test build (state effect)
  Given the app runs on localhost
  And the presenter booked F1-07 for 2026-10-22 as "Alex Morgan"
  When the app is opened with "?reset-demo"
  Then the booking of "Alex Morgan" is gone
  And the data equals a fresh seed for today

Scenario: Reset parameter ignored in the deployed build (DEC-031)
  Given the deployed app holds the booking of "Alex Morgan" for F1-07 on 2026-10-22 in this browser
  When the deployed app is opened with "?reset-demo"
  Then the booking of "Alex Morgan" is still there

Scenario: Reset parameter removed after the reset
  Given the app runs on localhost
  And the app was opened with "?reset-demo" and the seed was loaded
  When the presenter books F1-12 for 2026-10-22 as "Alex Morgan" and reloads the page
  Then the address bar has no "?reset-demo"
  And the booking of F1-12 is still there

Scenario: Seed on a weekend
  Given now is Sat 2026-10-24 12:00 office time
  When the seed is loaded
  Then D0 is Mon 2026-10-26 and none of its bookings is checked in

Scenario: No visible reset control
  When the employee looks through every screen
  Then there is no button or link to reset the data
```
