## Story
As the HR Business Partner, I want the app to say that names are self-reported, so that nobody relies on it for an emergency headcount.

## Context and business rules
1. Notice text: **"Names are self-reported — not for emergency headcount."** — REQ-029, DEC-012 `[W3 · Action items]` "flag the identity-trust gap (C3) as a known limitation, not a build item"
2. The notice is shown on the booking form, on the cancel and check-in name fields, and wherever names are shown (Variant B desk grid). — REQ-040, DEC-023
3. No headcount or "who is in the building" list is built. — DEC-012 (REQ-020 not built)

## Acceptance criteria
```gherkin
Scenario: Notice on the booking form
  Given the employee opens the booking form for F1-07 on 2026-10-22
  Then the notice "Names are self-reported — not for emergency headcount." is shown next to the name field

Scenario: No headcount list
  Given bookings exist for today
  When the employee looks through every screen of the app
  Then no screen lists who is in the building today

Scenario: Notice on the cancel and check-in name fields
  Given "Alex Morgan" booked F1-07 for 2026-10-22
  When the employee selects F1-07 to cancel or to check in
  Then the notice is shown next to the name field

Scenario: Notice wherever names are shown
  Given the names switch is On and a booking with consent is shown on the grid
  Then the notice is shown on that screen

Scenario: No notice needed where no names are shown
  Given the names switch is Off
  When the employee views the counts or the "Tomorrow" screen
  Then no names are on the screen (the notice is not required there)
```
