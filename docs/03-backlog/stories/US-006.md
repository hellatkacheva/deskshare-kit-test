## Story
As an employee, I want to be told clearly that a floor is full for a day, so that I pick another day or floor instead of hoping for a desk.

## Context and business rules
1. A floor is full for a date when all its desks are booked (Floor 1: 30, Floor 2: 25). — REQ-024, REQ-004, REQ-005
2. On a full floor no new booking is possible — a hard stop, not a warning. — REQ-011 `[W2 · Capacity limits]` "the system should refuse the booking outright, not just flag it"; DEC-004
3. The employee is told to pick another day or floor. — REQ-012 `[W2 · Capacity limits]` "the employee is told to pick another day or floor"
4. No waitlist. — DEC-005, OOS-006
5. Booked desks on a full floor stay selectable for cancel and check-in. — ASM-012
6. At the moment of saving, the app re-reads the bookings in this browser and re-checks that the floor is not full (another tab may have booked the last desk). — REQ-011, REQ-023 (finding #11)
